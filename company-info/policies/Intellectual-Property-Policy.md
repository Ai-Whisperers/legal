# AI Whisperers E.A.S. - Intellectual Property Policy

**Effective Date:** [Date]
**Version:** 1.0

---

## 1. PURPOSE

This Intellectual Property (IP) Policy establishes ownership, use, and protection of intellectual property created by or for AI Whisperers E.A.S. It ensures proper management of IP rights while respecting the rights of employees, clients, and third parties.

---

## 2. SCOPE

This policy applies to:
- All employees and contractors
- Partners
- Work created during employment/engagement
- Company resources and assets
- Client projects and deliverables

---

## 3. DEFINITIONS

### 3.1 Intellectual Property

**Includes:**
- **Copyrights:** Software code, documentation, content, designs
- **Patents:** Inventions and processes
- **Trademarks:** Brand names, logos, slogans
- **Trade Secrets:** Confidential business information, algorithms, methods
- **Know-how:** Technical knowledge and expertise

### 3.2 Work Product

All work created in the course of employment or engagement with AI Whisperers, including:
- Software and applications
- Documentation and manuals
- Designs and specifications
- Training materials
- Marketing content
- Research and analysis

### 3.3 Pre-Existing IP

Intellectual property created before employment or outside the scope of work for AI Whisperers.

---

## 4. OWNERSHIP OF INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

### 4.1 Company-Owned IP

**AI Whisperers owns all IP created:**

**During Employment/Engagement:**
- Work performed during work hours
- Using company resources
- Within scope of employment/contract
- Related to company business
- Funded by company

**Specific Examples:**
- Custom client deliverables (subject to client agreements)
- Internal tools and frameworks
- Marketing materials
- Business processes
- Training programs

### 4.2 Employee/Contractor Pre-Existing IP

**You retain ownership of:**
- IP created before engagement
- Personal projects outside work scope
- Created without company resources
- Unrelated to company business

**Disclosure requirement:**
- List pre-existing IP at start of employment
- Update list as applicable
- Avoid incorporating into company work without disclosure

### 4.3 Client-Owned IP

**Per client agreements:**
- Custom deliverables typically transfer to client upon payment
- Specified in service agreements
- Full rights assignment or license as agreed
- Client owns their proprietary data and materials

---

## 5. WORK FOR HIRE

### 5.1 Legal Doctrine

Under "work for hire" principles:
- Employer owns work created by employees in scope of employment
- Contractors' work may be work for hire if properly agreed
- Applies automatically in many jurisdictions

### 5.2 Contractual Agreements

All employees and contractors must sign:
- Employment/contractor agreement with IP assignment clause
- Acknowledgment of this policy
- Specific project IP agreements as needed

---

## 6. ASSIGNMENT OF RIGHTS

### 6.1 Employee Assignment

**Employees agree to assign to AI Whisperers:**
- All work product created during employment
- Related to company business
- Using company resources
- During or outside normal work hours (if related)

**This includes:**
- Present and future rights
- All copyright, patent, trademark rights
- Moral rights (where waivable)
- Rights to derivative works

### 6.2 Contractor Assignment

**Contractors agree:**
- Specific deliverables ownership as per contract
- Work for hire provisions
- Assignment of all rights in deliverables
- No rights retained unless specified

### 6.3 Documentation

**Proper assignment requires:**
- Written agreement
- Clear identification of IP
- Signatures of authorized parties
- Consideration (payment/employment)

---

## 7. CLIENT INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

### 7.1 Client Deliverables

**Default approach:**
- Custom work transfers to client upon full payment
- Specified in Master Service Agreement
- SOW identifies deliverables
- Assignment documented

**Rights transferred:**
- Copyright in custom code
- Rights to use and modify
- Rights to derivative works
- Documentation and materials

### 7.2 AI Whisperers Retained Rights

**Company retains:**
- Pre-existing IP and tools
- General methodologies and frameworks
- Reusable components
- Know-how and expertise

**Client receives:**
- License to use embedded AI Whisperers IP
- Non-exclusive rights
- For client's internal business use
- Cannot redistribute or sublicense

### 7.3 Open Source in Client Projects

**Disclosure:**
- Identify any open source components
- Disclose licenses (MIT, GPL, Apache, etc.)
- Client accepts open source terms
- Document in deliverables

---

## 8. PROTECTION OF INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

### 8.1 Confidentiality

**Protect company and client IP:**
- Do not disclose without authorization
- Use only for authorized purposes
- Protect from unauthorized access
- Follow information security policy

### 8.2 Copyright Protection

**Copyright notice:**
- © [Year] AI Whisperers E.A.S. All rights reserved.
- Include in source code, documentation
- Register important copyrights

**Protection measures:**
- Access controls on repositories
- NDAs with third parties
- Proper licensing
- Monitor for infringement

### 8.3 Trade Secrets

**Maintain confidentiality:**
- Do not disclose proprietary methods
- Secure confidential information
- Mark as confidential
- Limited access (need-to-know)

**Examples:**
- Algorithms and processes
- Client lists
- Pricing strategies
- Business plans

### 8.4 Trademarks

**Company trademarks:**
- "AI Whisperers" name and logo
- Service marks
- Brand elements

**Protection:**
- Consistent use
- Trademark notice (™ or ®)
- Monitor for infringement
- Register important marks

### 8.5 Patents

**Patentable inventions:**
- Novel technical solutions
- Non-obvious innovations
- Useful applications

**Process:**
- Disclose inventions to Partners
- Evaluate patentability
- File applications if valuable
- Maintain confidentiality before filing

---

## 9. USE OF THIRD-PARTY IP

### 9.1 Respect Third-Party Rights

**Prohibited:**
- Copyright infringement
- Unauthorized use of patented technology
- Trademark misuse
- Trade secret misappropriation

### 9.2 Open Source Software

**Guidelines:**
- Review license before use
- Comply with license terms (attribution, copyleft, etc.)
- Document open source components
- Obtain approval for copyleft licenses (GPL, etc.)

**Approved open source licenses:**
- Permissive: MIT, Apache, BSD
- Copyleft: Review with Partners before use

### 9.3 Licensed Software and Tools

**Requirements:**
- Use only properly licensed software
- Comply with license terms
- No unauthorized copying or sharing
- Maintain license inventory

### 9.4 Stock Images and Content

**Proper licensing:**
- Purchase appropriate licenses
- Respect usage restrictions
- Attribution if required
- No unauthorized downloads

---

## 10. OPEN SOURCE CONTRIBUTIONS

### 10.1 Personal Open Source

**Employees may contribute to open source:**
- On personal time
- Using personal resources
- Unrelated to company work
- No company confidential information

**Restrictions:**
- Do not use company code without approval
- Do not use company name/email (use personal)
- No company proprietary information
- Review for conflicts of interest

### 10.2 Company Open Source

**AI Whisperers may open source:**
- Non-sensitive tools and libraries
- With Partners approval
- Under appropriate license
- With proper documentation

**Process:**
1. Identify code for open sourcing
2. Review for confidential information
3. Choose appropriate license
4. Obtain Partners approval
5. Prepare documentation
6. Publish and maintain

## 13. DEPARTING EMPLOYEES/CONTRACTORS

### 13.1 Upon Termination

**Obligations:**
- Return all company property
- Destroy confidential information
- Cease use of company IP
- Reminder of continuing obligations

**Reminder:**
- IP assignment survives termination
- Confidentiality continues
- Non-compete if applicable
- No retention of company IP

### 13.2 Knowledge and Experience

**Departing individuals may:**
- Use general knowledge and skills
- Apply expertise gained
- Use publicly available information

**May not:**
- Use company trade secrets
- Disclose confidential information
- Copy or use proprietary materials
- Misappropriate company IP

## 14. IP DISPUTES AND ENFORCEMENT

### 14.1 Suspected Infringement

**If you discover infringement of company IP:**
1. Document the infringement
2. Notify Partners immediately
3. Do not contact infringer directly
4. Preserve evidence

**Company will:**
- Investigate
- Assess options
- Take appropriate action (cease and desist, litigation, etc.)

### 14.2 Claims Against Company

**If accused of IP infringement:**
1. Do not admit liability
2. Notify Partners immediately
3. Preserve all related documents
4. Cooperate with legal counsel

### 14.3 Indemnification

**Client indemnification:**
- AI Whisperers indemnifies clients for IP infringement in custom deliverables (as agreed in contracts)
- Subject to terms and limitations in agreements
- Client must notify promptly
- AI Whisperers controls defense
