# AI Whisperers E.A.S. - Legal Templates

This directory contains standard legal agreement templates for AI Whisperers E.A.S.

---

## Available Templates

### 1. NDA-Template.md
**Non-Disclosure Agreement (Mutual)**
- Use before discussing confidential business matters
- Protects both parties' confidential information
- Standard 3-year confidentiality period

### 2. Independent-Contractor-Agreement.md
**Contractor Engagement Agreement**
- For hiring freelancers and contractors
- Includes IP assignment and confidentiality
- Work-for-hire provisions

### 3. Master Service Agreement (../contracts/)
**Client Services Agreement**
- Located in contracts/ directory
- Comprehensive client engagement template
- SOW-based project structure

---

## Usage Guidelines

### Before Using Templates:

1. **Legal Review:** Have lawyer review for your jurisdiction
2. **Customization:** Insert specific terms (rates, timelines, scope)
3. **Parties:** Complete all party information
4. **Signatures:** Both AI Whisperers partners must sign

### Best Practices:

- Execute agreements BEFORE starting work
- Keep signed copies securely stored
- Track expiration dates
- Update templates annually
- Document any amendments in writing

## Required Modifications

**Always customize:**
- [ ] Party names and addresses
- [ ] Specific services/deliverables
- [ ] Payment terms and amounts
- [ ] Timeline and milestones
- [ ] Governing law (if client outside Paraguay)
- [ ] Special client requirements

**Review carefully:**
- IP ownership provisions
- Limitation of liability
- Indemnification scope
- Termination rights

## Storage and Management

### Document Management:
- Store signed agreements securely
- Use consistent naming: [Type]-[Party]-[Date].pdf
- Maintain both digital and physical copies
- Backup regularly

### Tracking:
- Contract register/database
- Renewal dates
- Amendment history
- Related documents (SOWs, change orders)

---

## Legal Considerations

### Paraguay Law:
- All templates drafted for Paraguay jurisdiction
- May need modification for international clients
- Tax implications vary by client location

### Multi-Jurisdictional:
- Specify governing law clearly
- Consider international arbitration
- Review local requirements
- Currency and payment method
