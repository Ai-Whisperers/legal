#### Structures Compared
1. **E.A.S.** (Entidad Autónoma de Servicios) - **RECOMMENDED**
2. **S.R.L.** (Sociedad de Responsabilidad Limitada)
3. **S.A.** (Sociedad Anónima)

#### Analysis Covers
- Formation requirements and costs
- Capital requirements
- Governance structures
- Tax obligations
- Compliance requirements
- Advantages and limitations
- Best use cases

#### Why E.A.S. for AI Whisperers?

✅ **Service-Optimized** - Specifically designed for professional services  
✅ **Cost-Effective** - Lower formation ($300-550) and maintenance costs  
✅ **Perfect for Partnership** - Ideal for 2-3 partners (50/50 ownership)  
✅ **International Ready** - Can serve global clients without restrictions  
✅ **Limited Liability** - Partners protected from company debts  
✅ **Tax Efficient** - 10% corporate tax rate  
✅ **Simplified Compliance** - Less bureaucracy than S.R.L./S.A.  

#### Key Decision Factors
- Service-based business model (AI consulting, development, training)
- Equal 50/50 partnership structure
- International operations focus
- Bootstrap capital requirements ($6,448 USD)
- No immediate plans for 4+ partners
- Remote/digital service delivery

## 📊 Quick Comparison

| Feature | E.A.S. | S.R.L. | S.A. |
|---------|--------|--------|------|
| Min Partners | 2-3 | 2+ | 2+ |
| Min Capital | ~$5,000 | ~$5,000 | ~$20,000 |
| Formation Cost | $300-550 | $500-1,000 | $1,000+ |
| Best For | Professional Services | General Business | Large/Public Cos |
| Complexity | Low | Medium | High |
| International Recognition | Medium | High | Highest |

**Our Choice:** E.A.S. - Perfect fit for our service-based, equal-partnership structure.

## 🔄 Future Considerations

### When to Convert to S.R.L.
- Adding a 4th+ partner (E.A.S. limited to 3)
- Seeking international investment (S.R.L. more recognized)
- Expanding into regulated industries
- Seeking more international credibility

### Growth Path
Currently, E.A.S. supports:
- ✅ Hiring employees as needed
- ✅ Opening international branches
- ✅ Serving unlimited international clients
- ✅ Scaling services without structural changes
- ✅ All planned business activities
