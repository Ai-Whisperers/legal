**1. Agreement Structure**
- Master agreement framework
- Statement of Work (SOW) system for individual projects

**2. Services & Scope**
- AI consulting services

**6. Warranties & Disclaimers**
- Professional workmanship warranty
- Service availability disclaimers

- Third-party service disclaimers

**7. Liability Limitations**
- Liability cap (fees paid in 12 months or $10,000)
- Consequential damages exclusion
- Allocation of risk

**8. Legal Provisions**
- Term and termination
- Dispute resolution
- Governing law (Paraguay)
- Independent contractor status
- Amendment procedures

**9. SOW Template (Exhibit A)**
- Project description
- Deliverables specification
- Timeline and milestones
- Fees and payment schedule
- Acceptance criteria

## 🎯 Usage

### For New Clients

1. **Review & Customize**
   - Update hourly rates (currently placeholder)
   - Adjust payment terms if needed
   - Add client-specific provisions

2. **Initial Discussion**
   - Share template with potential client
   - Discuss any modifications needed
   - Negotiate commercial terms

3. **Create SOW**
   - Use Exhibit A template
   - Define specific project scope
   - Set clear deliverables and timeline
   - Establish payment schedule

4. **Execution**
   - Both parties sign Master Agreement
   - Execute initial SOW
   - Additional SOWs can be added without re-signing master agreement

### For Ongoing Clients

- Create new SOWs for additional projects
- Reference master agreement for standard terms
- Only sign new SOW (master agreement remains in effect)

## ⚠️ Before Using

### Customizations Needed

1. **Rates & Fees**
   - Set hourly rates for different roles
   - Establish fixed-price project minimums
   - Define retainer pricing

2. **Company Details**
   - Update to official company name once registered
   - Add RUC (tax ID) once obtained
   - Include banking details for payments

3. **Legal Review**
   - Have Paraguayan lawyer review
   - Consider international law review for foreign clients
   - Add jurisdiction-specific clauses if needed

### Optional Additions

- Professional liability insurance references
- Industry-specific compliance requirements
- Data protection/GDPR provisions for EU clients
- Export control provisions for US clients
