## Key Advantages of This Structure

1. **Service-Optimized:** E.A.S. specifically designed for professional services
2. **Cost-Effective:** Lower formation and maintenance costs vs. S.R.L./S.A.
3. **Equal Partnership:** Perfect for 50/50 ownership
4. **International Ready:** Can serve global clients
5. **Limited Liability:** Partners protected from company debts
6. **Flexible Operations:** Can work remotely, serve international clients
7. **Tax Efficient:** 10% corporate tax rate
8. **Simplified Compliance:** Less bureaucracy than larger structures

---

## Important Considerations

### Limitations
- Maximum 3 partners (sufficient for current needs)
- If planning to add 4+ partners, must convert to S.R.L.
- Less internationally recognized than S.A.

### Requirements
- Both partners must sign major documents (joint administration)
- Physical address required in Paraguay
- Spanish required for official documents
- Annual compliance obligations

### Growth Path
- Can hire employees as needed
- Can open branches internationally
- Can convert to S.R.L. if expanding beyond 3 partners
- Can scale services without structural changes
